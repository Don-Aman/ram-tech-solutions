02⬡

Multi Tenant SaaS · Brand: Tavola

Tavola QR Menu Platform

One Next.js + Convex backend serving many restaurants, each reached by its own URL slug. Diners scan a QR to browse; staff edit their menu in a passcode protected admin; and every client can get a bespoke, custom built storefront with per brand theming.

  • QR ordering
  • Per brand theming
  • Master console
  • Real time
03◉

Marketplace · Commerce

RIBZ Design Marketplace

An apparel marketplace where Ethiopian designers upload artwork, it's printed on garments, and customers buy and pay via Telebirr or CBE. A printer fulfils each order through a print → ship → deliver pipeline, and designers earn an automated payout per sale.

  • Designer uploads
  • Cart & checkout
  • Local payments
  • Fulfilment pipeline
  • Payouts
04✚

Pharmacy · Cross Platform App

Pharmacy Management System

A cross platform Flutter app for pharmacies, running on desktop and mobile. Manage inventory and stock, record sales, and act on smart alerts for expiring, expired, out of stock and low quantity items. A visual dashboard turns daily activity into charts, with role based access for admins and pharmacists and full sales and general reports.

  • Inventory & stock
  • Sales & reports
  • Expiry alerts
  • Low stock alerts
  • Dashboard charts
  • Roles
