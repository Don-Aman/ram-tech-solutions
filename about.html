We build the systems that run your business.

Omedla Tech Solutions is a product-engineering company based in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. We design, build and operate production-grade software for real businesses, not slideware.

Our story

Omedla Tech Solutions started with a simple conviction: Ethiopian businesses deserve software engineered to the same standard as anything shipping anywhere in the world. We build ERP systems, multi-tenant SaaS platforms, commerce marketplaces and custom applications that run day-to-day operations for pharmacies, restaurants, clinics, barbershops and distributors.

Every platform on our products page is live and serving real users. We own the whole build, from the database schema to the pixel, and we stay on to run and improve what we ship.
